NutriScan - Food Calorie & Ingredient Scanner - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how AVTS Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app NutriScan - Food Calorie & Ingredient Scanner (package com.avtsstudio.foodscan), referred to here as "the app".

1. No account, data stays on your device

The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. Your settings, food log, scan history and any food-name corrections you make are stored only on your device. We do not have servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

2. Camera and photos

The app uses the camera, or a photo you pick from your gallery, only to recognise food, read barcodes and read ingredient labels. This recognition (food recognition, barcode scanning and text recognition) runs on your phone. Photos are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. The camera is used without audio, and the app does not record your voice.

3. Food database lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode or type a food name, the app sends that barcode number or search text to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, open food database, to get nutrition and ingredient details. No photo or personal information is sent. Open Food Facts' privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and can change their choice in the app's privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Paid features such as Remove Ads are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own a purchase so it can unlock features and restore purchases.

6. Sharing

If you tap Share, the app opens Android's share sheet with the content you choose to send. Nothing is shared without your action.

7. Health information

Nutrition and calorie figures are estimates for general information and are not medical advice.

8. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.

9. Children

The app is a general food and nutrition app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

10. Your choices and deleting data

Because your data is kept on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings > Apps > NutriScan > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. For advertising data held by Google, use your device's ad settings or Google's privacy tools.

11. Security

Network requests made by the app use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
