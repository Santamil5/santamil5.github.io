Belly Burn Home Workout – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how AVTS Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app Belly Burn Home Workout (package com.avtsstudio.bellyburn), referred to here as "the app".

1. No account, data stays on your device

The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. Everything you enter or create in the app, including your level, gender choice for the trainer, workout history, streaks, weight and waist logs, water intake, reminders, settings and progress photos, is stored only on your device. We do not have servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it. Progress photos you pick are copied into the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and can change their choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Remove Ads and Belly Burn Pro are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own a purchase so it can unlock features and restore purchases.

4. Notifications

If you turn on workout or water reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Android may ask for notification permission. No reminder data leaves your device. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.

5. Voice coach (text-to-speech)

The voice coach uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. The app sends it only the exercise instructions to read aloud. It does not record or listen to your voice, and it does not use the microphone.

6. Sharing

If you tap Share, the app opens Android's share sheet with a progress card that you choose where to send. Nothing is shared without your action.

7. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.

8. Children

The app is a general fitness app for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

9. Your choices and deleting data

Because your data is kept on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings > Apps > Belly Burn > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. For advertising data held by Google, use your device's ad settings or Google's privacy tools.

10. Security

Ad traffic to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
