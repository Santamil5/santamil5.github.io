DreamCanvas AI - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how AVTS Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app DreamCanvas AI (package com.avtsstudio.imagine), referred to here as "the app".

1. No account, creations stay on your device

The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. Your settings, credits, creation history and the images and videos you create are stored on your device. If you tap Save, the item is saved to your phone's gallery. We do not have servers that receive this data, and we cannot see it.

2. How images and videos are created

Some images are created entirely on your phone. Other images, image edits and videos are created by free third-party AI services, and to do this the app sends them only what is needed for that creation:

  • Your text prompt and chosen settings, to image services such as Pollinations (pollinations.ai) and AI Horde (aihorde.net).
  • Your prompt and, for image editing or photo-to-video, the photo you choose, to public AI demos hosted on Hugging Face Spaces (huggingface.co).

These services receive your request directly from your phone, along with your IP address as part of any internet connection, and handle it under their own privacy policies. Please do not include personal information in prompts, and only upload photos you have the right to use. Background removal runs on your phone and does not upload your photo.

3. Content safety

The app filters prompts to block adult and harmful content. Blocked prompts are not sent to any service. Finished images and videos are also checked on your phone, and anything flagged is deleted. A record of blocked requests is kept only in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may indicate approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and can change their choice in the app's privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Credit packs and the DreamCanvas Pro subscription are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play which purchases you own so it can add credits, unlock Pro and restore purchases. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store.

6. Sharing

If you tap Share, the app opens Android's share sheet with the creation you choose to send. Nothing is shared without your action.

7. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

9. Your choices and deleting data

Because your data is kept on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Settings > Apps > DreamCanvas AI > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. For advertising data held by Google, use your device's ad settings or Google's privacy tools.

10. Security

Network requests made by the app use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
